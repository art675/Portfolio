\mbox{$[+45/-45/0/+45/-45/0/0/-45/+45/0/-45/+45]$}.

\textbf{Flange layups.}
Top flange: \mbox{$[90/0_6/90/0_6]$}. Bottom flange:
\mbox{$[90/0_6]$}, where $0_6$ denotes six consecutive 0-degree plies.
