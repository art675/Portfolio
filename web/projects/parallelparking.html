Project 13 · Section 1

Project Framing / Vehicle Platform

03 AUTONOMY / / / PROJECT 13

Learning-platform vehicle • • • Ultrasonic ranging • • • Finite-state parking logic • • • Physical validation

This project focused on a compact autonomous parking routine for the learning-platform vehicle.

Ultrasonic measurements were used to identify open space beside the car, while a finite-state controller separated the maneuver into driving, scanning, positioning, parking in, and done states.

The objective was not only to detect a usable space, but to convert that measurement into a repeatable physical parking sequence.

THE VEHICLE PLATFORM

The parking routine was implemented on the same scaled vehicle platform used throughout the autonomy work.

The top view highlights the compact packaging and the proximity-sensing hardware used to reason about nearby obstacles and available parking space.

THE DESIGN QUESTION

How can a vehicle detect a long-enough open region while moving, remember where that space begins and ends, and then trigger a parking maneuver only after it is positioned correctly?

The next stage turns the ultrasonic measurements into this decision signal.

Project 13 · Section 2

Ultrasonic Sensing / Open-Space Detection

TURNING ULTRASONIC RANGE INTO OPEN-SPACE EVIDENCE

Raw range signal • • • Side-space detection • • • Spatial interpretation

The parking decision begins with a simple measurement: how far away is the nearest surface beside the vehicle?

A blocked region produces a short range, while a sudden increase in measured distance indicates that the sensor is looking into open space.

Tracking that change as the vehicle moves provides the evidence needed to estimate where a parking gap begins and where it ends.

RAW ULTRASONIC RANGE DATA

The recorded ultrasonic trace contains long plateaus, abrupt drops, and repeated returns.

Rather than treating every individual sample as a command, the controller used the signal as evidence of blocked versus open space.

Persistence and vehicle motion were therefore important: a parking space had to remain open long enough to represent a physically usable gap.

WHAT THE SENSOR REPRESENTATION ADDS

The spatial rendering makes the same range information easier to interpret: clusters of returns correspond to nearby surfaces, while gaps between those returns indicate free space.

This gives a useful visualization of the geometric signal the parking logic is trying to extract from the raw sensor stream.

Project 13 · Section 3

Finite-State Parking Logic

FROM OPEN-SPACE DETECTION TO A PARKING MANEUVER

Finite-state control • • • Space detection • • • Positioning • • • Parking execution

Once open-space evidence was available, the maneuver was organized as a finite-state machine.

Separating the task into discrete states made the behavior easier to reason about: the vehicle could continue driving while searching, transition into scanning when a candidate gap appeared, position itself relative to that gap, execute the parking motion, and finally terminate in a known done state.

HOW THE STATES DIVIDE THE PROBLEM

DRIVING moves along the parking row.

SCANNING evaluates whether the side sensor remains in open space.

POSITIONING places the vehicle at the correct starting point for the maneuver.

PARKING_IN executes the parking sequence, and DONE provides a terminal state after the maneuver.

Project 13 · Section 4

Physical Validation / Successful Parking

AUTONOMOUS PARALLEL PARKING

Space detection • • • State transitions • • • Physical maneuver • • • Successful demo

The final test combined sensing, state transitions, and vehicle motion on the physical parking setup.

The vehicle first identified the available space, continued until the correct maneuver starting point was reached, and then executed the parking sequence between the neighboring vehicles.

PROJECT OUTCOME

The completed routine demonstrated the full parking workflow on hardware: detect an opening, confirm that it is long enough, transition through the parking states, and execute the maneuver physically.

The state-machine structure kept sensing and motion logic interpretable throughout the test.
