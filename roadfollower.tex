\textbf{\textcolor{DeepGreen}{Thresholding the detector}}

\vspace{0.08in}

Pixel-count distributions were used to distinguish three practical cases:

\vspace{0.09in}

\textbf{Too few pixels} --- the expected lane feature was not reliably visible.

\vspace{0.07in}

\textbf{Valid range} --- sufficient edge information was present for the
controller to use.

\vspace{0.07in}

\textbf{Too many pixels} --- the window likely contained excessive scene
structure or noise.

\vspace{0.12in}

This converted raw image features into discrete detection flags that could
drive higher-level control logic.
};
